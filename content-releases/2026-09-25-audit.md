# Portal question audit — 25 September 2026

Audited the structure and answer-key formats of all 4,955 pre-existing question records across EST, EST II, SAT and EST English. This is a structural audit, not a claim that every historical answer or diagram was independently solved.

## Database changes applied

- 306 existing records corrected. Categories overlap: 123 appended topic labels removed from choices; 30 trailing chapter/set headings removed; 117 fraction/decimal key strings converted to accepted-answer arrays; eight multi-solution strings converted to arrays; 26 letter keys replaced by independently solved written responses; 12 multiple-choice formats restored (including two source-verified graph items); six incomplete or ambiguous records marked held and excluded from scoring.
- Six EST questions independently solved, visually checked against `Math_Question_Bank_Ghoneem_Clean.pdf`, and released: HOA 072 (page 48), PSD 004 (148), PSD 145 (256), PSD 172 (271), PSD 205 (295), PAM 078 (355).
- Original review-source text remains intact. Derived wording/domain/rounding clarifications are recorded in each released question's revision metadata. PSD 004 includes its complete five-row source table.
- One previously rejected correct fraction response was credited; its completed assessment score changed from 14/31 to 15/31. Timing and review-access timestamps were preserved.
- Prior question/key states and release-source states are preserved in database audit-log entries named `question_correction_20260925`, `question_release_20260925`, and `grading_correction_20260925`.
- Changes used guarded transactions and rejected edits to records in active exams.

## Frontend changes

The mistake notebook, daily quiz and weak-topic drill called nonexistent `renderAssets`, silently suppressing every graph/table. They now call the existing exam `figure` renderer directly. Dashboard content runs the same math rendering pass as exams, with support for parenthesized and bracketed LaTeX delimiters in student and instructor views.

## Verification

- Eight automated tests pass, including the regression test that loads the real exam renderer into all three practice panels and checks image-to-question mapping and hidden answer keys.
- 720 database image references inspected: all 720 references across the custom domain and existing Sites alias have corresponding source files; all 456 supplied hashes for those local references match.
- Post-write queries: zero missing keys; zero MCQ keys absent from their choices; all six releases have keys and no hold; fraction acceptance and wrong-response rejection pass.
- Two remaining letter-valued grid-in records are already held for missing original graph/source review. Other unresolved source-review entries were not bulk released.
- An authenticated student browser walkthrough was not performed; local Chromium is unavailable. Image-file presence/hash checks and renderer regression tests do not certify every historical diagram's mathematical content.

No changes to the separate ledger database.
