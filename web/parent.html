My child’s progress

Your five-minute weekly check-in
  1. Check what is still due. Pick one assignment and agree when to finish it.
  2. Open a completed assessment. Ask your child to explain one answer before reading the worked solution together.
  3. Ask “Which step would you change?” Have them retry the question independently.
  4. Compare scores within the same topic. One missed question is a reason to review that idea. Look for repeated difficulty before calling it a weakness.
  5. Elapsed time is the duration the portal stored. It can include pauses. Missing timing is shown as “Not recorded.”
