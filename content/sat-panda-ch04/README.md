# College Panda workbook: Chapter 4 (other types of equation questions)

40 source-numbered questions are in the SAT bank and Final Revision: Exercise 1 Q1–19 and Exercise 2 Q1–21. The original image of every prompt and its choices is attached. Prompts are on PDF pages 52–57; printed solutions on PDF pages 324–327. Equation identities, absolute values, real-root counts, and extraneous roots were checked against the source and solved independently.

Exercise 1 Q2 asks for *one possible* solution to an absolute value equation; its private key accepts either valid root, −12 or −28.

Build reproducibly with `python build.py ORIGINAL_PDF OUTPUT_SQL`. The builder validates the exact PDF checksum, exercise numbers, existing questions, keys, and revision links. The source scan and generated SQL are not committed.
